FAQ

Got questions?

Is GoalScroll free?
GoalScroll will offer a free tier with core features. We're still finalizing pricing for premium features, but our goal is to keep it accessible to everyone. Join the waitlist to get early-bird pricing when we launch.
Can I bypass the app blocker?
Nope. GoalScroll uses Apple's ScreenTime API at the system level. You can't bypass it by deleting the app, restarting your phone, or switching accounts. The block stays until you complete your habit and submit proof. That's the whole point.
What counts as proof?
Depending on the habit, you can submit a photo (e.g., you doing pushups), a screenshot (e.g., a meditation app session), or a written reflection. Our AI analyzes the proof to verify you actually did the habit — not just took a random picture.
Do I need to use my camera?
Not necessarily. While photo proof is one option, you can also use screenshots from other apps or write a reflection. We offer multiple proof methods so you can choose what's comfortable for you.
Is my data private?
Absolutely. Photos and proofs are processed for verification and are not stored permanently. We don't sell your data, and habit data stays on your device. Your privacy is a core design principle, not an afterthought.
When will GoalScroll launch?
We're currently in beta testing and working toward an App Store launch soon. Join the waitlist to be the first to know when we go live and to get early access.
